Relational Research

Research that adds generational value

Relational research and accountability take time, commitment, and resources, which is often a challenge because of both researcher and institutional barriers.

However, community-engaged research rooted in relationship, respect, responsibility, and reciprocity serves Indigenous communities well beyond a grant cycle, seeking to add generational value.
