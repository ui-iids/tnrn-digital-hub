Inter-Institutional Research Protocols

Each Tribe and institution has similar yet unique frameworks, values, and relationships that guide their research and data governance decisions.

Our Commitment

Responsible, reciprocal, respectful research relationships

Through a collective commitment to strengthening our inter-institutional relationships, these protocols are being developed for Tribal nations and institutions in ways that support the responsible, reciprocal, respectful research relationships we are committed to building, strengthening, and upholding together.

These Native-serving protocols are established through Native advisory boards, memoranda of understanding (MOUs), committees, and faculty and staff positions. This page directs you to each partner’s website describing its Indigenous research partnerships, protocols, and practices as they currently stand.
