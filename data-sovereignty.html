Indigenous Research & Data Sovereignty

Tribal nations hold the inherent right to govern research with their communities and the data, knowledge, and stories that come from it. This space supports institutions and Tribes in putting that right into practice.

Support by audience

For Institutions

Practices and principles that help universities conduct research responsibly with Tribal nations and support Indigenous scholars.

  • FAIR, CARE, and OCAP principles
  • Supporting Indigenous students
  • Supporting Indigenous faculty

For Tribes

Learning and technical assistance to strengthen Tribal research governance and partnerships on Tribal terms.

  • Courses and certificates
  • Designing sovereignty-affirming research partnerships
  • Data sovereignty technical assistance

Key Principles

FAIR, CARE, and OCAP

These complementary frameworks guide how research data is managed, shared, and governed.

FAIR

Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable. Principles that make research data easier to discover and use.

CARE

Collective Benefit, Authority to Control, Responsibility, Ethics. People- and purpose-oriented principles for Indigenous data governance.

OCAP®

Ownership, Control, Access, Possession. Principles asserting First Nations’ control over data collection and use.
