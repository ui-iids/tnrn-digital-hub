Tribal Nations Research Network

Recentering knowledge exchange between Tribes and Idaho universities, in support of Tribes’ sovereignty and self-determination.

Critical to that effort is the inclusion of Indigenous ways of knowing into higher education and research. The network focuses on collaboration between Tribes and universities through the development of research originated by Tribal nations.

Our Goals

Two commitments at the center

  • Build Tribe-centered research capacity

  • Build Idaho education institutions’ capacity to center Tribe-driven research

  • Researchers

    Tribal nation researchers, faculty, and student researchers, including Indigenous students, working together.

  • Training System

    Building Tribal administrative and institutional capacity through a professional development framework sustained within institutions.

  • Inter-Institutional Capacity

    Shared systems, procedures, and agreements, plus a sustainable training model and an inter-institutional website that lasts beyond I-CREWS.

  • SEED Grants

    Piloting collaborative research practices, including Coeur d’Alene Tribe projects on temperature and snowpack scenarios and a Coeur d’Alene Basin integrated archival framework.

  • Relational Research

    Research rooted in relationship, respect, responsibility, and reciprocity that serves communities well beyond a grant cycle.
